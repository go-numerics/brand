# go-numerics — brand assets

Official logos for the **go-numerics** organization.

<img src="avatar/go-numerics.png" width="160" alt="go-numerics">

The mark is a quadrature: an integral sign beside the area under a curve, cut
into panels the way a numerical rule cuts it. Its gradient (`#B794E4` to
`#7445AD`) is a violet that sets go-numerics apart from the emerald of
go-ndarray, go-fft and go-notebook and from go-plot's blue; its dark end reads
on white at 6.57:1 and its light end on a dark page at 7.70:1.

## Formats

- **SVG** — `svg/color`, `svg/white` (white, transparent), `svg/black` (black, transparent)
- **PNG** 16→1024 px, 3 variants — `png/<variant>/<size>/`
- **JPG** (color, white background) — `jpg/<size>/`
- **ICO** (Windows) — `ico/`
- **ICNS** (macOS) — `icns/`
- **GitHub avatar** 512 px — `avatar/`
- **Social preview** 1280×640 (repo banner) — `social/`

`check/kit.py` counts the whole matrix on every pull request.
